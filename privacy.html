Privacy policy

Fitness Mailbox is a private app used only by its owner.

Google data accessed

The app requests only the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. It reads and writes files exclusively in its own hidden application data folder in the user's Google Drive. It cannot see any other files in Drive.

How data is used

The files in that folder are encrypted end to end (AES-256-GCM) on the user's own devices before upload. The key exists only on those devices. Google and third parties cannot read the contents.

The data is used solely to sync the user's own devices.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, transferred to third parties or used for advertising. The app runs no servers of its own and has no analytics.

Retention and deletion

Files are deleted after processing. The user can remove all app data at any time in Google Drive (Settings, Manage apps, Fitness Mailbox, Delete hidden app data) and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
